AI Guide

5 Practical Ways to Use AI Every Day

AI can be useful for brainstorming, summarizing your own notes, learning difficult concepts, drafting outlines and organizing tasks.

1. Brainstorm ideas

Give AI a clear goal and ask for several approaches. Pick the useful ideas and improve them yourself.

2. Learn a topic

Ask for a beginner explanation, examples and practice questions. Always verify important facts.

3. Create outlines

Before writing an article or video script, use AI to turn your main points into a logical outline.

Mobile Guide

How to Keep Your Android Phone Fast

Keep storage from becoming unnecessarily full, remove apps you no longer use, install system updates and restart the phone when it behaves unusually.

For important files, keep a backup. Avoid installing unknown APKs because they can create security risks.
